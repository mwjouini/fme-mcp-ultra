# Support Policy

Welcome to the `fme-mcp-ultra` support hub.

## Community Support Channels

- **GitHub Discussions**: For general questions, architectural guidance, and spatial data pipeline discussions: [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/mwjouini/fme-mcp-ultra/discussions).
- **GitHub Issues**: For bug reports and verified regressions: [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/mwjouini/fme-mcp-ultra/issues).
- **Documentation**: Extensive guides and client setup recipes are available in the [Documentation](https://mwjouini.github.io/fme-mcp-ultra/).

## Enterprise Support

For mission-critical production deployments requiring SLAs, custom transformer adapters, or enterprise FME Flow federation, please contact the maintainers directly.
